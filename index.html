Engineering Methodology

From initial blueprint to handover

Disciplined project governance from initial architectural brief to key delivery.

  1. Stage 01

    Client Brief & Site Demarcation

    In-depth alignment on client functional requirements, plot orientation, budget governance, and timeline milestones.

  2. Stage 02

    BPMS Tracing & Municipal Approvals

    MahaBPMS / AutoDCR digital submission, town planning scrutiny, FSI calculations, and statutory municipal sanctioning.

  3. Stage 03

    3D Elevation & RC Structural Design

    Photorealistic 3D facade modeling, lighting studies, and IS-compliant RC structural framing with Bar Bending Schedules.

  4. Stage 04

    Rigorous On-Site Construction

    Earth excavation, RCC footing, column casting, floor slabs, precision brick masonry, and structural framing under continuous engineer presence.

  5. Stage 05

    Multi-Tier Quality Testing

    Concrete cube compression testing, plumbing pressure verification, electrical continuity, and waterproofing certification.

  6. Stage 06

    Finishing & Turnkey Handover

    Final architectural finishing, formal occupancy documentation (OC), and post-occupancy structural warranty support.
