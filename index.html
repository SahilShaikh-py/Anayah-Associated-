Our Work Steps

From first plan to handover

Clear and careful project management from the first talk to key handover.

  1. Stage 01

    Your Needs & Site Marking

    We understand your needs, plot direction, budget and time plan in detail.

  2. Stage 02

    BPMS Drawing & City Approvals

    Online MahaBPMS / AutoDCR filing, town planning check, FSI calculation and official city approval.

  3. Stage 03

    3D Front View & Concrete Design

    Real-looking 3D front views, light studies and IS-based concrete frame design with steel bar plans.

  4. Stage 04

    Careful Site Construction

    Digging, concrete footings, columns, floor slabs and neat brickwork — with an engineer on site all the time.

  5. Stage 05

    Quality Checks at Every Level

    Concrete cube strength tests, pipe pressure checks, electrical checks and waterproofing proof.

  6. Stage 06

    Finishing & Final Handover

    Final finishing, the occupancy certificate (OC) papers, and structure warranty help after you move in.
